# Third-party literary material

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to QuizIQ's original software, tests, documentation and interface assets. It does not claim authorship of, or independently relicense, the literary excerpts.

QuizIQ includes 16 short verbatim excerpts from these original human-authored works:

| Author | Work | Original-text edition used |
|---|---|---|
| Lewis Carroll | Alice's Adventures in Wonderland | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/lewis-carroll/alices-adventures-in-wonderland/john-tenniel) |
| Lewis Carroll | Through the Looking-Glass | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/lewis-carroll/through-the-looking-glass/john-tenniel) |
| Oscar Wilde | The Importance of Being Earnest | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/oscar-wilde/the-importance-of-being-earnest) |
| Oscar Wilde | Lady Windermere's Fan | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/oscar-wilde/lady-windermeres-fan) |
| Mark Twain | Pudd'nhead Wilson | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/mark-twain/puddnhead-wilson) |
| Jerome K. Jerome | Three Men in a Boat | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/jerome-k-jerome/three-men-in-a-boat) |
| William Shakespeare | Hamlet | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/william-shakespeare/hamlet) |
| Jane Austen | Pride and Prejudice | [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/jane-austen/pride-and-prejudice) |

`quote-sources.json` identifies every exact excerpt, author, work, chapter or act, character/speaker when relevant, and direct source URL. The app also displays these credits in its Literary shelf. `quote-verification.txt` records checks against the source texts, and `tools/verify_sources.ps1` can repeat those checks online.

Keep the attribution and source metadata when distributing the included excerpt catalog. No Standard Ebooks cover artwork, illustrations, branding, or full ebook files are included. Windows system libraries are supplied by the operating system and are not redistributed in the source repository.
